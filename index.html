04 Session format

How would you like to run your session?

Zoom is simpler. We handle the recording. Self-record gives you flexibility.

If you choose Zoom

Here's what happens

Choose your preferred date and time below. 24 hours before your session, both you and your partner will receive an email with the full session details and Zoom link.

Your partner will also receive the case script at that point — they're expected to read and prepare. You (the candidate) will only receive your case information at the start of your session. You will have 5 minutes to read through it before your 15-minute station begins, exactly as you would in the real exam.

At the scheduled time, you and your partner join the Zoom. The meeting auto-records to our cloud. Once you're done, simply leave the meeting and we'll handle the rest.

If you choose self-record

Here's what happens

24 hours before your chosen recording date, your partner will receive their case materials by email. They are expected to read and prepare in advance.

On the day of your recording, you will receive your case information by email. Do not open it until you are set up and ready to begin.

You (the candidate) must not open or read the case documents until you are ready to record your station. Opening the case early compromises the integrity of the mock — you'd be testing your ability to rehearse, not perform.

Record the station on your own device (phone camera or laptop webcam). Start the recording, open your case information, and take 5 minutes to read through it. When you are ready, say "starting now" on camera and run the 15-minute station. Each station is 20 minutes in total. When done, send the video to us.
